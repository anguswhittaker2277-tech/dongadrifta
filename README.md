## Voice credits

Madman's, the backpackers' and Dima's voices were generated with [Kokoro-82M](https://github.com/hexgrad/kokoro)
(Apache-2.0), using phonemes from espeak-ng. See `tools/voices/README.md` for how the voice pack is made.
Matt's voice was made in Voicebox from his own voice profile (the recordings are in `tools/voices/rec_mt/`).

## The BMW

The BMW is an E70 X5, from the Sketchfab model ["1999 BMW X5"](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/1999-bmw-x5-4d504448006b417ba6193d35adbe446d)
(an E70 despite the name), converted to `e70.glb` with its driver figure and its own wheels taken out. The paint is matched to
the owner's own car, the interior is mirrored to right-hand drive, and the 20" Style 214 wheels and tyres are made in the game.

## The cotton picker

The John Deere CP690 / 7760 round-module cotton picker (`cp690.glb`) is from the Farming Simulator 25 mod
**"John Deere Roller Pickers 2008-2021"** by **Jolly Farms Modding** (v1.2.1.0), built on GIANTS Software's CP690.
Its model, sticker sheet, module and wrap textures, animations and moving parts come from the mod; the tyres, rims,
beacon and sounds are made in the game. The `.i3d.shapes` decoder in `tools/fs-mod/` is a Python port of
[I3DShapesTool](https://github.com/Donkie/I3DShapesTool) by **Donkie** (MIT). See `tools/fs-mod/README.md`.

## The 9RX

The John Deere 9RX quad-track (`rx9.glb`) is from the Farming Simulator 25 mod **"John Deere 9RX High-Horsepower
Series"** (US configuration), converted with `tools/fs-mod/build_vehicle.py`. The tracks, which are a base-game asset
in FS, are built in the game.
